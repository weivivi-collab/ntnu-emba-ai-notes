經理人決策視角EXECUTIVE LENS

P.01 / AI ENABLEMENT

Vibe coding 與 Agent：從工作需求出發，先描述成果與驗收，再討論自動化。Vibe coding & agents: start with the work, define outcomes and acceptance, then consider automation.

經理人決策視角EXECUTIVE LENS

以決策權、交付責任與可驗證成果設計職務；讓 AI 支援工作，而非模糊責任。Design jobs around decisions, delivery and measurable outcomes. Let AI support work without obscuring accountability.
